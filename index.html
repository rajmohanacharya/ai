Recommended prompt formula

Include as many of these elements as relevant. Clear instructions generally produce stronger, more useful answers.

TaskState the exact outcome you want.
Files / InputName the files, data, text, or examples to use.
ContextExplain the audience, situation, and why it matters.
RequirementsList mandatory features, checks, or sections.
ConstraintsSpecify budget, tools, versions, length, deadline, or exclusions.
OutputChoose the format: table, plan, script, email, summary, checklist, and so on.
ValidationAsk for assumptions, uncertainties, evidence, risks, and verification steps.
